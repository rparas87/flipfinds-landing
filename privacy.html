Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

FlipFinds is built with a "Privacy-First" philosophy. We believe your sourcing data and business inventory should belong to you, and you alone.

1. Data Collection

FlipFinds does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not have access to your email, name, or location data.

2. Local Storage

All data you enter into the app—including scanned items, profit calculations, and inventory lists—is stored locally on your mobile device. We have no way to access this data.

3. External Data Requests

To provide pricing information, the app may perform lookups against public market data. These requests are anonymous and do not include any user-identifying information.

4. Contact Us

Questions? Contact us at: support@parasdigital.net
