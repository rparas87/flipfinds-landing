Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlipFinds show sold comps?
FlipFinds is designed for speed. It provides a market snapshot based on active listings (median active price). While sold prices are important, FlipFinds helps you decide quickly whether an item is worth deeper research.
Who is FlipFinds for?
Resellers and thrifters who want to make faster buy decisions and keep a clean, simple record of their inventory and profit.
Do I need an account?
No. FlipFinds works completely without an account. All your data is stored locally on your mobile device.
